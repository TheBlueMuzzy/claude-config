# Discover — explore wide (diverge)

Goal: understand the space before narrowing. More options, not fewer. Size it to the game — a one-screen arcade toy needs a short chat, not a research report.

## Do
1. **Ask** what the idea is, what excites Muzzy about it, who'd play it, and where (phone on the bus, table with friends).
2. **References** — 3–5 games in the space (fewer if Muzzy knows it well). For each, break it down with **mda-analyze** reference: what players feel → what they do → which rules cause it. Note what to steal and what to avoid. Web research only when it helps.
3. **Feel** — draft 1–3 rough experience targets in players' words (**mda-analyze** targets — loose; /define locks them).
4. **Hunches** — a rule or odds question worth checking? A quick **proto** sim.
5. **Directions** — 2–4 rough takes on the core loop, one line each. Don't pick yet.

## Output
- Create `.planning/GDD.md` from `~/.claude/config/bmuz/templates/GDD.md`; fill §1 Pitch (rough), §2 targets (rough), §9 open questions. Header: `Current phase: Discover`.
- Longer notes (reference breakdowns, directions) → `.planning/research/discovery.md`.
- Commit `GDD: discover`.

## Done when
- A rough pitch, 1–3 rough targets, references broken down, 2–4 directions on the table.
